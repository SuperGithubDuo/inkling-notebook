Install guide

Install in about two minutes

This beta isn't code-signed yet, so you'll see a security prompt the first time. Signing is how Windows and macOS confirm who made an app. Without it, your computer can't verify the publisher and asks you to confirm.

Only install Inkling from this page or its GitHub release, and don't install it on a school- or work-managed computer without permission. If you're unsure, don't install it and ask us.

  1. Open the downloaded Inkling-Setup-…-x64.exe (usually in your Downloads folder).
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, check the file name and Unknown publisher, then click Run anyway.
  3. Inkling installs for your account only (no administrator password), adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts, and opens.
  1. Open the downloaded Inkling-….dmg and drag Inkling into Applications.
  2. Open Inkling from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the developer and won't open it yet. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Inkling, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password. You only do this once.

System requirements

What you need

WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit. ARM PCs (Surface Pro X, Snapdragon laptops) run it through Windows' built-in emulation.
MacmacOS 12 Monterey or later, on Apple silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel.
InternetOnly for the AI tutor and update checks. Notebooks work offline.
Pen or touchOptional. A mouse or trackpad works too.
Phones and tabletsNot supported yet: no iPad, Android or web version.
Your notebooks, your computer

Private by design

  • Your notebooks are saved on your computer as you write, with a backup every day. There's no account and no cloud storage.
  • The AI tutor only sees what you ask about, after you click Allow. Please don't send personal information.
  • The tutor is free, with about 10 answers a day per computer. AI answers can be wrong, so check them.
  • Under 18? A parent or guardian should install Inkling and decide about the AI.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking, in the app or on this page.
